# 盲测题 3

**场景**：加拿大温哥华 Horseshoe Bay 渡轮码头的候船厅，晚上九点，大雾。最后一班去纳奈莫的渡轮取消了。码头边的汽车旅馆只剩一间房，前台说十分钟后给答复。四个陌生人在候船厅里等，互相不认识，家人都不在身边，可以打电话。舞台提示里没有性别和说话人线索。

**四份档案**：
- ../../examples/01-底特律修理工-AAVE.md（Darnell）
- ../../examples/02-纽约诉讼律师.md（Rachel）
- ../../examples/03-伦敦老钱画商-英国绅士.md（Julian）
- ../../examples/04-昆士兰加油站老板娘.md（Kerry）

**任务**：下面 12 句台词，每人 3 句，已打乱、去掉名字。括号里是动作和说话对象，不透露说话人和性别。只根据四份档案，判断每句是谁说的，并写出依据（用了档案的哪一条，排除了谁）。

1. *(sitting back down, to the room in general)* I drove up this morning and then I sat in the car for two hours and then they said it was full and then they said it was cancelled and now we're all fighting over one room.
2. *(to the last one who came in, eyes still on a phone)* You walked in at eight-forty. After the announcement. You read the board on your way in. Correct?
3. *(without standing, to the one holding the list)* Perhaps we could first ask whether anyone here actually needs the room, before we hand it to whoever happened to sit down earliest.
4. *(on the phone, to someone at home, voice low)* Boat's cancelled. Fog. I'm good, baby — go on to bed.
5. *(raising a hand, to the one holding the list)* Suits me, love. I've been sitting here since seven.
6. *(to the one who suggested asking about need)* Noted. The list stands.
7. *(to the one holding the list)* No. I'm not doing nine hours in a plastic chair.
8. *(on the phone to someone at home, turned toward the window)* Darling, it's perfectly fine — there's fog, there's no boat, and four of us are deciding one bed by committee.
9. *(standing, tearing a page from a notebook, to all three)* Here's what we're doing. Names on a list, in the order we came in. The list goes to the desk.
10. *(to everyone, after a long pause)* Yeah nah, this is going nowhere. Someone toss a coin.
11. *(quietly, to the one asking the questions)* I done told you that. Twice.
12. *(to the one who suggested the coin)* I'm afraid that won't be possible — I've never let a coin decide where I sleep, and I don't intend to begin in a ferry terminal.
