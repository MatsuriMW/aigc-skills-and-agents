# 盲测题 2

**场景**：美国底特律机场 A38 登机口，晚上 11 点 40 分，冰暴。最后一班去纽约拉瓜迪亚的航班延误，停机坪上一辆除冰车趴窝了；候补只剩一个座位，地勤走开了。四个陌生人在等。

**四份档案**：`../../examples/01-底特律修理工-AAVE.md`（Darnell）、`02-纽约诉讼律师.md`（Rachel）、`03-伦敦老钱画商-英国绅士.md`（Julian）、`04-昆士兰加油站老板娘.md`（Kerry）。

**任务**：下面 12 句台词，每人 3 句，已打乱、去掉名字。括号里是动作和说话对象，不透露说话人和性别。只根据四份档案，判断每句是谁说的，并写出依据（用了档案的哪一条，排除了谁）。不要看答案文件。

1. *(offering a bag of snacks to the group)* Go on, take a handful, love. Bit stale. Beats the twelve-dollar sandwich.
2. *(on the phone, to someone at home)* Are you in bed? In bed, or on the bed with the iPad? That's what I thought.
3. *(to the person who got the seat, as boarding is called)* Do enjoy the flight. I'm told LaGuardia is lovely at this time of night.
4. *(about the standby seat)* There's nothing to settle. I'm first on the list. I checked at nine-fifteen. You didn't.
5. *(at the window)* That de-icer ain't moving. Hydraulic pump. Been whining since ten.
6. *(to the person at the window)* You can hear a pump. Through that glass. Fifty yards out. Correct?
7. *(to everyone)* Forty minutes, the bloke at the desk reckoned. Said it at nine, and then he went off for smoko and that's the last we saw of him.
8. *(about the one standby seat)* I wonder whether we might settle the standby seat like grown-ups, rather than by whoever gets to the desk first.
9. *(to the person who just claimed first place on the list)* How very organised of you. I'd rather assumed it was a queue, but do carry on.
10. *(as a second de-icing truck pulls up, to the person who predicted it)* My husband was like you. Hear a truck coming and tell you what was crook with it before it even pulled in.
11. *(on the phone, to someone at home)* Baby. Still at the airport. Ice. No, eat. Don't wait on me.
12. *(still facing the window)* Don't need to see it. That's a pump running dry. They gonna bring a whole other truck. You watch.
